In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

Each hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Great Falls HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Great Falls and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Great Falls HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep for peak demand.

Next to an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Clients who switch to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
