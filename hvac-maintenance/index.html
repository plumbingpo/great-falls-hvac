The Benefits of Professional HVAC Maintenance

Changing filters, clearing the outdoor unit, keeping registers open, and checking the drain are the homeowner's share of maintenance, and they matter. Beyond those, it takes test instruments. Failures that leave you without heat or air rarely look like anything: an 80 percent capacitor resembles a healthy one, and a low charge is visible only in superheat and subcooling. A crack in the heat exchanger, a lazy flame signal, a pressure switch near its limit, or an amp draw drifting toward the breaker rating all stay hidden until someone measures them, and each is less expensive to handle a season early.

Maintenance Tips for HVAC Maintenance

Smart maintenance is almost always cheaper and easier than an early replacement. Put these technician-approved steps to work around your property:

  • Pay attention to new noises or longer cycles and mention them at your next visit
  • Give the condenser room to breathe and rinse its coil lightly each spring
  • Replace your filter on a one- to three-month cycle; nothing else you do pays off more
  • Get your tune-ups on the calendar during mild weather, well before the rush
  • Check carbon monoxide alarms monthly in any home with fuel-burning appliances
  • Resist closing vents in spare rooms; the system is designed with all registers open
  • Flush the condensate line at the start of cooling season before algae blooms in it
